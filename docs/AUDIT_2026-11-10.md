# The Mist Village: project audit, 10 November 2026

This is Milestone 1 of the master production brief. It was gathered from the code, the story data, the docs and the
test runs made today. Nothing here was changed to produce it. "Measured" means a number from a run, and "not measured"
means nobody has a number yet.

## 1. Project health

**Overall: a playable, complete Part 1 with a strong world, held back by presentation, audio and release
plumbing.**

| Area | State |
|---|---|

### Errors seen in today's runs

- `Parameter "material" is null` (12 in the choice-2 run). Some mesh has a freed or missing material, which the
  renderer keeps asking about.
- `timeout waiting for fence` (Metal). This happened when two copies of the game shared the GPU. Running alone it
  didn't appear, so it isn't a game bug, but it shows how close the M1 runs to its limit.
- `Interpolated Camera3D triggered from outside physics process` in the drive world. A warning only, but it can cause
  camera jitter.

## 4. Keep as they are

- The story, its 19-evidence structure and the court system: six points answered with found evidence, bought evidence
  objected to, and the night replayed on the map.
- The interactive set pieces: bank slip and cash bundles, delivery and OTP, doorbell and DVR footage, keypad PIN,
  evidence wall, phone (contacts, camera, recorder), table and hands, the jail chapter.
- Bail rules: signing at 6–7 PM, strikes, lates, suspicion. Never an unavoidable game over.
- Real Coimbatore from OpenStreetMap, with real public place names and fictional private businesses.
- Driving on the left; Indian signals; earth roads in the villages; the ghat with its nine hairpins.
- The two-wheeler balance and lean, the gearbox and CVT models, the engine sound synthesis.
- The story test runner (`--storyskip`, `--storychoose`, `--storyshots`) and the vehicle and movement labs.
- English and Tamil on every line, with Noto Sans Tamil (OFL) and Inter (OFL), licences in `assets/fonts`.

## 5. Technical risks

- **The M1 is the floor.** Every visual gain has to be tested on Low on this machine, and graded up for real PCs. No
  Windows or Linux machine has run the game yet.
- **Two worlds, three traffic systems.** A fix in one doesn't reach the others (today's elephant stop is drive-world
  only).
- **Very large scripts** make changes riskier. Every change to story.gd needs a story run.
- **Save format.** The story save is versioned and finds steps by signature. The drive and city saves need the same
  care before any change.
- **Shared folder.** Other sessions edit the HUD, map and faces files. I don't touch those.
